## 1. Getting started

1. Open the BeatMind AI invite link and sign in with Google or your email. We send a one-time code to your email.
2. Confirm you are 18 or older. During the beta, BeatMind is for adults only.
3. Choose your language: English or हिन्दी. You can change it any time in Settings.
4. Pick what you want to make: **For my video**, **Song from my lyrics** or **Beat**.

During the beta, everyone has a daily limit on new songs. The limit is shown on the Create screen.

## 2. Make a song from a vibe

1. Go to **Create → For my video**.
2. Describe the feeling, for example "calm lo-fi for a rainy Mumbai vlog" or "energetic dhol beat for a wedding reel".
3. Choose the length, whether you want vocals, and the language.
4. Select **Generate**. You get two versions, called takes.
5. Listen to both. Select **Keep** on the one you like, or **Regenerate** to try again.

Tips: name the mood, the setting and the tempo ("slow", "fast"). Do not name real singers or songs; those requests are blocked (see article 13).

If a take fails or is held back by our safety checks, you get your credit back automatically.

## 3. Turn your lyrics into a song

1. Go to **Create → Song from my lyrics**.
2. Paste your lyrics in Hindi (Devanagari), Hinglish (Hindi in English letters) or English, up to 3,000 characters. Or select **Write lyrics for me** and give a topic and mood.
3. Mark sections such as `[Verse]`, `[Chorus]` and `[Bridge]` if you want a specific structure.
4. Choose a style and a voice type, then select **Generate**.

Languages marked "experimental" may mispronounce some words. We only fully support languages that passed our listening tests.

## 4. Make a beat

1. Go to **Create → Beat**.
2. Choose the genre, tempo (60 to 200 BPM) and key, and the instruments to include or leave out.
3. Select **Generate** and keep the take you like.

## 5. Download your song

Open the song and select **Download**.

| Plan | What you can download |
| --- | --- |
| Free | MP3 for personal listening. MP3 for commercial use for up to 5 songs a month (article 7). |
| Creator and Pro | MP3, WAV and four separated parts (vocals, drums, bass, other) for every song you make on the plan |

Separated parts are made by splitting the finished song, so a little of one part can be heard in another.

## 6. Who owns my song?

> Counsel review required before publishing.

- **Songs made while you are on a paid plan:** BeatMind transfers to you all the rights BeatMind holds in the song. You can use it anywhere, including commercially. BeatMind keeps only the permission it needs to store, process and show the song to you and people you share it with. We use your songs to improve our models only if you turn on that option.
- **Songs made on the free plan:** BeatMind keeps the rights. You may listen to them personally, and you may use up to 5 a month commercially with attribution (article 7).
- **Ownership is fixed when the song is made.** Upgrading later does not change songs you already made on the free plan.
- **If you cancel,** you still own and can download the songs you made while paying.
- **If a payment is reversed** (for example a chargeback), songs made in that period go back to the free-plan rules.

**What we cannot promise.** In some countries, music made by AI without enough human creative input may not be protected by copyright at all. BeatMind can transfer the rights it has, but it cannot guarantee that copyright exists in a song or that no one will ever make a claim. Your own lyrics and creative choices are recorded in the song's history, which may help show your contribution.

## 7. Using free songs commercially

> Counsel review required before publishing.

- On the free plan you can choose up to 5 songs each calendar month for commercial use, for example in monetised videos.
- Open the song and select **Use commercially**. You receive a licence certificate.
- You must credit BeatMind where you publish, for example in your video description: `Music: made with BeatMind AI`.
- The licence lets you use the song; it does not transfer ownership.

## 8. Why every song is labelled as AI-generated

Indian rules in force since 20 February 2026 require AI-made audio to be clearly labelled and to carry provenance information that is not removed. The EU has similar rules. So every BeatMind song:

- shows an "AI-generated" label on its page and player,
- carries a label and signed content credentials inside the file,
- contains an inaudible watermark,
- is recorded in our provenance history.

Removing these labels or markers is against our terms and may break the law where you publish.

## 9. Verify a song

Anyone can check whether a file or certificate came from BeatMind at **Verify** (no sign-in needed). Upload the file (up to 20 MB) or enter the certificate ID. Detailed information is shown only for public songs or to the certificate holder.

## 10. Plans, prices and credits

- Prices are shown in your local currency and adjusted for the cost of living where you pay. The price you pay is based on your payment method's country.
- Credits are used when you generate. Failed or held-back takes are refunded automatically.
- In India you can pay by UPI, card or net banking.

Exact prices will be published before paid plans open.

## 11. Cancel your plan

Go to **Settings → Plan → Cancel plan** and confirm. It takes no more than three steps. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for. You keep the songs you own.

## 12. Your data: export and delete

- **Export:** Settings → Privacy → Download my data. You get your account details, song history and files.
- **Delete:** Settings → Privacy → Delete my account. We delete your account and songs within 30 days. We keep a pseudonymised record that a song was AI-generated, because the law requires provenance records; it no longer identifies you.
- **Withdraw consent** for analytics, marketing or model training at any time in Settings → Privacy.

## 13. What you cannot make

- Songs that copy or imitate real artists, singers or existing songs, or use their names.
- Hate, harassment, threats, or content that targets religion, caste or community.
- Sexual content involving minors, in any form.
- Fake songs that impersonate real people, including politicians.
- Bulk uploads to streaming services to game royalties.

Blocked requests show the reason. If you think a block is wrong, select **Appeal**; a person reviews it within 72 hours.

## 14. Report a song or a rights problem

> Counsel review required before publishing.

If a BeatMind song uses your work, voice or name without permission, or breaks the law, select **Report** on the song page or write to the grievance officer (article 17). Include your contact details, the song link, and what is wrong. We acknowledge reports within 24 hours. Government and court orders are acted on within 3 hours as Indian rules require.

## 15. If you get a copyright claim on YouTube

> Counsel review required before publishing.

BeatMind cannot remove claims on YouTube or other platforms. If your BeatMind song is claimed:

1. Open the song in BeatMind and download its licence certificate and history (Pro and Creator include a dispute pack).
2. Check the claim. If the claimant owns similar music, the claim may be valid; tell us using **Report** so we can investigate.
3. If you believe the claim is wrong, dispute it on the platform using the certificate and history as evidence.

## 16. Accessibility

BeatMind aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. The player, editor and creation screens work with a keyboard and screen readers, lyrics are always shown as text, and nothing plays automatically. Tell us about any barrier through **Help → Accessibility feedback**.
