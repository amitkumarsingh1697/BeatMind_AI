# BeatMind AI · Client pitch deck technical notes

## 1. What was built and how

The deck is generated by code, so every slide can be rebuilt from source in one command. It is still a normal, editable PowerPoint file.

1. **Content.** `src/script_content.js` holds the following fields for each of the 23 slides: act, title, objective, key message, visual, layout, motion, transition, duration, presenter notes, talking points and the line that bridges to the next slide. The same data feeds four outputs:
   - the PowerPoint speaker notes;
   - `BeatMind_AI_Presenter_Script.docx`;
   - `BeatMind_AI_Storyboard.md`;
   - the running order.
2. **Visual assets.** `src/render_assets.js` uses Playwright and Chromium to place real Sprint 6 screenshots into browser, phone and card frames. It tilts them with CSS 3D perspective and lighting, then exports transparent PNGs at 2× resolution. It also renders the background plates, the glow orbs and the cover loop, frame by frame, which ffmpeg encodes to H.264. `src/tight_trim.py` trims each render to its body and feathers the edges.
3. **Slides.** `src/build_deck.js` uses pptxgenjs 4.0.1 to build:
   - a 16:9 widescreen layout (13.33 × 7.5 in);
   - two slide layouts: `BM_CONTENT`, which has the footer and slide number, and `BM_FULL`;
   - sections for each act;
   - native text, shapes, tables and two native charts;
   - alt text on every image;
   - speaker notes.

   It also writes `out/anim_plan.json`, which lists the entrance effect, order and delay for each object.
4. **Motion.** `src/postprocess.py` uses lxml to edit the PPTX XML directly, because pptxgenjs cannot write animations or Morph. It:
   - adds a Morph transition to each slide, with a Fade fallback for players that lack Morph;
   - adds Fade through black on the cover;
   - writes the `p:timing` tree for the entrance effects;
   - applies the brand gradient fills;
   - sets the cover video to autoplay, loop and stay muted;
   - removes duplicate media, which took the file from 28.8 MB to 9.3 MB.
5. **Checks.**
   - The deck passes the OOXML validator, which checks the schema, relationships, content types and charts.
   - The deck was rendered through LibreOffice to PDF and JPEG, and all 23 slides were inspected visually in two passes. The first pass found 9 layout problems and the second found 2; all were fixed and re-rendered.
   - The presenter script and the design guide were also rendered and checked page by page.

## 2. Motion: what is native, what is video and what is static

| Kind | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Native transition** | Morph (by object), 1.2 s. The glow orbs share names across slides, so they drift from one position to the next. Players without Morph fall back to Fade. | Slides 2 to 23 |
| **Native transition** | Fade through black, 1.0 s | Slide 1 |
| **Native entrance animation** | Fade, Float In (rises 6% of the slide height), Zoom (from 82%) and Wipe from left. All are set to "After previous" and staggered 0.1 to 0.28 s apart, so each slide builds on its own in about 1 to 2.5 s. The deck has 513 effects in total, and one click moves to the next slide. | Every slide |
| **Embedded video** | `cover_loop.mp4`: 8 s, 1920×1080, silent, autoplays and loops. It shows floating 3D devices, drifting light and equaliser bars. | Slide 1 |
| **Static 3D renders** | Perspective device frames and glass cards, with depth from shadow and glow. These are pictures and do not move by themselves; the entrance animations move them on screen. | Slides 1, 6, 8, 9, 11, 18, 23 |
| **Not used** | Interactive 3D (GLB models), sound, emphasis loops and motion paths. GLB models break in PDF, Google Slides and LibreOffice, and constant motion distracts in a sales conversation. | None |

## 4. Playback

**Best: PowerPoint for Microsoft 365, or PowerPoint 2019 or later, on Windows or macOS.** Everything works here: Morph, entrance builds, the looping video and the speaker notes in Presenter View. Before presenting:
- Install Manrope and Inter on the presenting machine. They are not embedded. Without them, PowerPoint substitutes a default font and some titles may wrap differently.
- Run the deck once in Slide Show so the video is cached.
- Advance with one click per slide. Wait for each build to finish before talking to the detail.

| Platform | What works | What does not |
|---|---|---|
| **PowerPoint 2016** | Entrance animations, video, notes | Morph falls back to Fade. |
| **PowerPoint for the web** | Morph and most entrance effects, notes, slide show | Video autoplay may need a click. Fonts fall back unless they are installed in the tenant. Present from the desktop app when you can. |
| **Keynote (Mac)** | Opens and plays the video | Morph, and some builds, become simple dissolves. Fonts must be installed. |
| **Google Slides** | Imports layout, text, images and notes | Morph becomes Fade or None, and most entrance effects are lost or simplified. The video is dropped and the poster image remains. Charts become images. Fonts may change. |
| **LibreOffice Impress** | Layout and most entrance effects | Morph shows as a plain cut or fade, and video playback depends on the system codecs. Use LibreOffice for viewing, not for presenting. |
| **PDF** (`BeatMind_AI_Client_Pitch_Deck.pdf`) | Shows the final state of every slide | **The PDF contains no motion.** No transitions, builds or video; slide 1 shows the video's first frame. Use it for email, print and pre-reads. |

## 5. Limitations

- **Real screens, test audio.** The screenshots are real, but the Sprint 6 build used test tones in place of model output. The waveforms are real UI, not songs. The deck says so on slide 8.
- **Modelled numbers.** Prices, margins and the GPU cost per take are modelled hypotheses from the BeatMind pricing and financial model (Oct 2026). Every slide that shows them is labelled as modelled or illustrative.
- **No proof of demand yet.** The deck shows no traction, customers or ROI, because none exist yet. Slide 15 gives a value formula for a partner to fill in with their own numbers.
- **Mixed source versions.** The project folder has PRD v1.0; the repository has v2.1. The deck follows v2.1 and the Sprint 6 build. Items that appear only in v1.0 are shown as roadmap.
- **LibreOffice QA renders.** The QA renders come from LibreOffice. PowerPoint's text layout may differ by a few pixels, and titles were sized with margin to allow for that.
- **Fonts not embedded.** Embedding them would need PowerPoint itself.

## 6. Editing

**Small edits in PowerPoint.** Text, tables, charts and colours are all native and editable.
- If you move an object on one slide, Morph still works, because it matches objects by name. Keep the `!!orb…` names as they are.
- To change an entrance effect: Animations, then Animation Pane.
- To swap a screen: right-click the picture, choose Change Picture, and keep the alt text.
- If you edit the PPTX by hand, those edits are lost on the next rebuild. Either make edits in source, or stop rebuilding once you start hand-editing.

**Edits in source** (recommended while the content is still changing):

| To change | Edit |
|---|---|
| Notes, talking points, timings, script | `src/script_content.js` |
| Slide content and layout | `src/build_deck.js` (one block per slide, marked `// N ── Title`) |
| Colours and fonts | The `C` and `F` objects at the top of `build_deck.js`, and the `COLORS` list in `build_design_guide.js` |
| Effect types and timings | the effect and delay arguments of each `anim(name, effect, delay, dur)` call in `build_deck.js`; the effect XML is in `postprocess.py` |
| Screens | Re-capture, then run `ASSETS=1 bash build.sh /path/to/new/screens` |

Rebuild everything with:

```bash
cd source
npm install
bash build.sh                      # deck, PDF, script, guide, storyboard
ASSETS=1 bash build.sh <screens>   # also re-crop screens and re-render 3D assets and video (about 5 min)
```

Outputs go to `source/out/`. Set `FONT_DIR` if the fonts are not in `~/.fonts`.
