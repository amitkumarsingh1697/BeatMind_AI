# Brand and name research

Version 0.1, 2026-10-28. Responds to DEC-019: keep BEATMIND AI unless a better, available, music-relevant name exists.

## 1. Method

1. Generate candidates from English and from Indian, Greek and Norse music traditions.
2. Check `.com` registration through the Verisign RDAP service [V, SRC-121, 2026-10-28]. An RDAP "not found" response means the domain was not registered at the time of the check. It does not mean the domain can be bought at the standard price, or that the name is free of trademarks.
3. Shortlist names that are easy to say in Hindi and English, are not already well-known brands, and have a free `.com` or a reasonable variant.
4. Trademark clearance (C-01) is required before any spend on branding. We could not query trademark databases from this environment.

`.ai` domains could not be checked from this environment [U]. Check at a registrar before deciding.

## 3. Shortlist and recommendation

| Name | Strengths | Weaknesses | Domain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **BeatMind AI** (keep) | Clear meaning worldwide; already used in the deck and repository; easy in English and Hindi | `beatmind.com` is taken; "beat" may suggest beats only, not songs | beatmindai.com or getbeatmind.com |
| **SwaraMind** | Indian root (*swara*, note) plus a global word; fits the India-first launch; distinctive | Less obvious meaning outside South Asia | swaramind.com |
| **Gandharva AI** | Strong mythological story about celestial musicians | Hard to spell and say outside India; "Gandharva" is used by many Indian music schools and businesses (trademark risk [H]) | gandharvaai.com |
| **Saptak AI** | Musically precise (the seven notes) | Little meaning outside India | saptakai.com |

**Recommendation:** keep **BeatMind AI** for the beta, using `beatmindai.com` (or `getbeatmind.com` if it is gone), because the name is already in use and works globally. Hold **SwaraMind** as the alternative if the trademark search (C-01) finds a conflict for "BeatMind" in classes 9 (software), 41 (entertainment) or 42 (SaaS). Buy the chosen `.com` soon: domain registrations of music-and-AI names are recent and frequent, as `naadai.com` (August 2026) and `dhunai.com` (October 2025) show [V].
