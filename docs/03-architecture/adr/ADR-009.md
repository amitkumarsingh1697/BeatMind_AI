# ADR-009: Payments: Razorpay in India, a merchant of record elsewhere

| Status | Date | Deciders |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed | 2026-10-28 | Founder and developers; drafted by Claude (delegated authority DEC-004, DEC-011) |

## Context

PPP pricing by billing country (DEC-013), UPI in India, and global sales tax handling with a tiny team.

## Options considered

1. Stripe everywhere.
2. Razorpay for India plus a merchant-of-record provider (Paddle or Lemon Squeezy) for other countries.
3. App-store billing only.

## Decision

Option 2, subject to an Indian entity (A-001) and to confirming each provider supports country-specific prices (C-04).

## Consequences

UPI and Indian methods through Razorpay; the merchant of record collects and remits VAT and sales tax abroad, which three developers cannot manage. Higher fees abroad are accepted for lower compliance burden.
