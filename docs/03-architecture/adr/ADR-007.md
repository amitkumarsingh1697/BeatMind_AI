# ADR-007: Model strategy: commercially licensed open-weight models, no foundation training in Phase 1

| Status | Date | Deciders |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed | 2026-10-28 | Founder and developers; drafted by Claude (delegated authority DEC-004, DEC-011) |

## Context

No training data or budget (DEC-005); cost must be near zero (DEC-004); outputs must be usable commercially by users who own them (DEC-009).

## Options considered

1. Train our own model.
2. Use only a paid API (Lyria 3, ElevenLabs, MiniMax).
3. Self-host open-weight models with commercial licences; paid API as fallback.

## Decision

Option 3. Primary: ACE-Step 1.5 (MIT per model card; claims licensed, royalty-free, public-domain and synthetic training data). Evaluate HeartMuLa and YuE (Apache-2.0, no data statement). Candidate for beats: Stable Audio 3.0 (Community Licence, licensed data). Fallback: Lyria 3 Pro API with a daily spend cap. Excluded: MusicGen and LeVo (non-commercial licences).

## Consequences

Very low cost per take (TDD 9.1) and no training spend. Training-data provenance is source-stated, not verified (RISK-001, A-024), so the terms and investor materials must disclose it and similarity screening is mandatory. Quality may trail Suno and Google (RISK-002, RISK-003); gate G1 decides launch languages. Re-evaluate after funding for licensed fine-tuning (FEAT-33).
