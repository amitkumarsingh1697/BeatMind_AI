# ADR-010: Provenance: hash-chained ledger, C2PA content credentials, AudioSeal watermark

| Status | Date | Deciders |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed | 2026-10-28 | Founder and developers; drafted by Claude (delegated authority DEC-004, DEC-011) |

## Context

India IT Rules (20 Feb 2026) require labels and permanent provenance markers on synthetic audio; EU Article 50 requires machine-readable marking (SRC-117, SRC-119). Users need proof of what they contributed.

## Options considered

1. Metadata labels only.
2. Watermark only.
3. Ledger plus C2PA plus watermark.

## Decision

Option 3. Every event is hash-chained per song; daily signed digest; C2PA manifest on every export; AudioSeal watermark carrying a take reference; public verify endpoint.

## Consequences

Each layer covers another's weakness: metadata can be stripped, watermarks can be attacked, the ledger cannot be removed from our side. Robustness targets are set in Batch 2 (TD-04).
