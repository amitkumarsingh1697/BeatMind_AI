# Acceptable Use Policy (draft)

> Draft for counsel review. Not legal advice. Version 0.1. Enforced by REQ-F-50, REQ-F-51, REQ-F-52 and the threat model abuse cases AB-01 to AB-08. Content rules for each market are reviewed by local counsel (BUG-087).

You must not use BeatMind to create, upload, share or attempt to create:

1. **Imitations of real people.** Songs that imitate or name a real artist, singer, band or public figure, or use their voice, style or likeness, without their written permission through a BeatMind licensing programme.
2. **Copies of existing works.** Existing songs, melodies or lyrics you do not have rights to, including by naming the song, pasting its lyrics, or describing it closely enough to reproduce it.
3. **Hate and harassment.** Content that attacks or demeans people on the basis of religion, caste, ethnicity, nationality, gender, sexual orientation, disability or similar characteristics, or that harasses, threatens or bullies anyone.
4. **Sexual content involving minors**, in any form. We report such content to the authorities.
5. **Violence and illegal acts.** Content that incites violence or terrorism, or promotes illegal activity.
6. **Deception.** Fake songs presented as real statements or performances by real people, including political figures; content intended to mislead voters or the public; removing or hiding AI labels.
7. **Platform abuse.** Bulk generation or uploading to streaming services to manipulate plays or royalties; using bots or multiple accounts to get around limits; reselling access.
8. **Security abuse.** Probing, scanning or attacking the service; attempting to access others' songs or accounts; reverse engineering models or watermarks; using the verify page to test watermark removal.
9. **Anything illegal** in India or where you live or publish.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity, we may block the request, withhold or remove the song, cancel certificates, limit or suspend the account, or close it. Serious illegal content is reported to the authorities. You can appeal decisions (Copyright and Takedown Policy, section 5).
