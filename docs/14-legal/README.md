## Drafting principles (fair to both sides)

1. **Users get what they pay for.** Paid users receive every right BeatMind holds in their songs, keep them after cancelling, and can export their data.
2. **BeatMind keeps only what it needs to operate**, plus model training only with opt-in consent.
3. **No promise BeatMind cannot keep.** The documents never say a song is protected by copyright or will never be claimed (REQ-F-26, COMP-US-01).
4. **Users stand behind what they put in.** Users warrant they have rights to their lyrics and inputs; BeatMind does not certify originality (BUG-046).
5. **Clear, fair limits on liability**, without excluding liability the law does not allow to be excluded.
6. **Plain language first.** Each section starts with a short summary.
