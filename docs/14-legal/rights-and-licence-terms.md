# Rights and Licence Terms (draft)

> Draft for counsel review. Not legal advice. Version 0.1. Implements DEC-008 and DEC-009; open points L-01 to L-03 and L-05 in the legal pack README.

**Summary:** If you make a song while you are on a paid plan, BeatMind gives you every right it has in that song, and keeps only what it needs to run the service. If you make a song on the free plan, BeatMind keeps it, you can listen to it personally, and you can use up to 5 songs a month commercially if you credit BeatMind. Copyright in AI-made music is uncertain in many countries, so BeatMind cannot promise a song is protected or will never be claimed.

## 1. Definitions

- **Song:** any audio, lyrics, stems and associated files generated for you by BeatMind, including edits.
- **Paid Song:** a Song created while your account had an active, paid plan whose payment for that period has settled.
- **Free Song:** any other Song.
- **BeatMind Rights:** whatever copyright, related rights and other intellectual property rights BeatMind holds in a Song, if any.

## 2. Paid Songs: assignment to you

2.1 BeatMind assigns to you all BeatMind Rights in each Paid Song, worldwide, for the full term of those rights, effective when the Song is created and the payment for that period settles.

2.2 BeatMind keeps a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to host, process, reproduce and display the Paid Song to provide the service to you and to people you share it with, to keep provenance and labelling records, and to comply with law. BeatMind will not sell, license or publish your Paid Song to others.

2.3 BeatMind will use a Paid Song to train or improve models only if you have opted in.

2.4 Your rights in Paid Songs continue after you cancel or close your account. If you close your account, download your songs first; we delete files as described in the Privacy Policy.

2.5 If the payment for a period is reversed or charged back, the assignment for Songs created in that period does not take effect, those Songs become Free Songs, and their certificates are cancelled.

## 3. Free Songs: BeatMind keeps rights; you get licences

3.1 BeatMind keeps the BeatMind Rights in Free Songs.

3.2 **Personal licence.** You may listen to and share Free Songs for personal, non-commercial purposes.

3.3 **Commercial licence.** In each calendar month you may choose up to [5] Free Songs for commercial use [A-005, Q-F05]. For each one, BeatMind grants you a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual licence to use the Song in your own works and channels, including monetised videos, podcasts, ads and live performances, provided you credit "Music: made with BeatMind AI" where your work credits appear.

3.4 Under the commercial licence you may not: register the Free Song with a content-identification system (such as YouTube Content ID) as your own; release the Free Song by itself on streaming services as your own recording; or sell or sublicense the Free Song by itself.

3.5 Upgrading to a paid plan does not convert Free Songs into Paid Songs. [Paid users may convert a Free Song to a Paid Song using [credits]; rule to confirm, REQ-F-24.]

## 4. What BeatMind does not promise

4.1 **Copyright may not exist.** In some countries, including the United States, material generated by AI without sufficient human creative control is not protected by copyright. Whether your Song is protected depends on the law where protection is claimed and on your own creative contribution. BeatMind assigns what it holds; it cannot assign rights that do not exist.

4.2 **No claim-free guarantee.** BeatMind screens Songs for close similarity to known recordings but cannot guarantee that a Song is free of third-party rights or that no platform or person will make a claim.

4.3 **Models and training data.** Songs are generated by AI models, some developed by third parties. BeatMind relies on the publishers' statements about how those models were trained and has not independently verified them. [Counsel: L-03.]

## 5. Your responsibilities

5.1 You promise that your Inputs (lyrics, prompts, files) are yours or that you have permission to use them.

5.2 You must keep the AI-generated labels and markers in the Song (Terms, section 6).

5.3 You are responsible for disclosing AI use where a distributor, platform or law requires it.
