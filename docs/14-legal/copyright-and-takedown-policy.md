## 2. Reporting content

Anyone can report a BeatMind song that infringes their rights (copyright, voice, name, likeness) or is otherwise unlawful, using **Report** on the song page or by email to the grievance officer. Please include:

1. Your name and contact details, and whether you act for a rights holder.
2. The song link or certificate ID.
3. The work or right you believe is infringed, with evidence of your rights.
4. A statement that the information is accurate and that you are authorised to act.

We acknowledge reports within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within [15] days, or faster where the law requires.

## 3. Government and court orders

Orders from a court or an authorised government agency under Indian law are acted on within 3 hours of receipt, as the IT Rules require for the relevant orders (COMP-IN-02). [Counsel: confirm which order types carry the 3-hour deadline.]

## 4. What happens to the song

When a report is valid on its face, we may disable the song (page, embeds and downloads), cancel its certificate, and notify the account holder with the reason, unless the law forbids notice. We keep evidence and provenance records.

## 5. Counter-notices and appeals

If your song was removed or blocked and you believe it was a mistake, select **Appeal** or reply to the notice. Explain why, with any evidence (for example, that the lyrics are yours). A person reviews appeals within 72 hours (REQ-F-59). Where a counter-notice procedure applies under law (for example the US DMCA), we follow it.

## 6. Repeat infringers

Accounts that repeatedly infringe others' rights will be closed.
