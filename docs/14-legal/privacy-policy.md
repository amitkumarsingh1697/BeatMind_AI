# Privacy Policy (draft)

> Draft for counsel review. Not legal advice. Version 0.1. Based on the [DPIA](../05-compliance/dpia.md). Structured for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and Rules 2025 (COMP-IN-04); add GDPR-specific sections before serving EU users (COMP-EU-02).

**Summary:** We collect what we need to run BeatMind: your account details, what you create, payment status and security data. We never sell your data. We use your songs to train AI only if you opt in. You can download or delete your data at any time.

## 1. Who is responsible

[BeatMind AI Private Limited], [address], is the data fiduciary (controller) for personal data processed through BeatMind. Contact: [privacy email]. Grievance officer: [name, email].

## 2. What we collect and why

| Data | Why | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email, Google account ID, display name, optional phone number | To create and secure your account | Your consent to use the service |
| Year and month of birth | To confirm you are 18 or older | Legal obligation |
| Country (from your connection and payment method) | Prices, taxes, legal rules for your country | Use necessary for the service; legal obligation |
| Prompts, lyrics, settings, songs, edits | To make and store your music | Your consent to use the service |
| Provenance records (what was created, when, which inputs were yours) | To label AI content and keep records the law requires | Legal obligation |
| Payment status and provider customer ID (no card or UPI details) | Billing | Use necessary for the service; legal obligation |
| IP address, device information, logs | Security and abuse prevention | Legitimate use for security |
| Product analytics | Improving the product | Your consent (off by default) |
| Your songs for AI training | Improving our models | Your explicit consent (off by default) |

## 3. Who we share data with

- Service providers that host, store, deliver, send email for, or analyse BeatMind, under contracts that limit their use of the data.
- Payment providers ([Razorpay], [merchant of record]) who process payments as independent data fiduciaries for payment data.
- Google, when you sign in with Google, and when a song is generated through Google's music model as a fallback. Only your creative inputs are sent, never your account details.
- Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data.

## 4. Where data is stored

Primarily in [India]. Some providers process data in other countries; transfers follow Indian law and contractual safeguards.

## 5. How long we keep data

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Account and songs | While your account is open; deleted within 30 days of account deletion |
| Provenance records | Kept after deletion in pseudonymised form, because the law requires AI provenance records; they no longer identify you |
| Security logs | 30 days |
| Payment and tax records | As long as tax law requires [counsel: period] |
| Analytics | 12 months, then aggregated |

## 6. Your rights

You can: get a summary and copy of your data (Settings → Privacy → Download my data); correct it; delete your account; withdraw consent for analytics, marketing or training; nominate a person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated [counsel: confirm process]; and complain to our grievance officer, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

We respond within [30] days. Grievances are acknowledged within 24 hours.

## 7. Security

We encrypt data in transit and at rest, limit staff access, require multi-factor authentication for staff, and test our security regularly. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

## 8. Children

BeatMind is for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that a child's data was collected, we delete it.

## 9. Changes

We will notify you of material changes before they take effect.
