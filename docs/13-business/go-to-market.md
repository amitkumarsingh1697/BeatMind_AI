# Go-to-market plan

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Version | 0.1 (Batch 4), 2026-10-28 |
| Market | India beachhead, English worldwide (DEC-101) |
| First customers | Short-form and YouTube creators, then independent artists, then producers (DEC-102) |
| Budget | Near zero before funding (A-002): organic, community and partnership channels only |
| Status | Draft. Funnel targets are hypotheses until the validation plan produces evidence. |

## 1. Positioning and message

**One line:** Original songs for your videos, in your language, that you can own, at an Indian price.

| Audience | Main message | Proof we can show (once built) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube and Reels creators | "Music for your video in a minute. Use it in monetised videos." | Licence certificate, AI label, dispute pack (FEAT-09, FEAT-11) |
| Independent artists and lyricists | "Hear your Hindi lyrics as a full song." | Hindi and Hinglish quality (gate G1 results) |
| Producers | "Beats and stems to start your next track." | Beat mode, stems (FEAT-04, FEAT-07) |

Rules for every message:

- Never claim "copyright-free", "no claims ever", "licensed training data" or "full copyright ownership" (REQ-F-26, BUG-018, RISK-011).
- Always show the AI-generated label in demos and ads.
- Compare with competitors only on facts we can cite with a date (price, language, downloads).

## 2. Launch phases

| Phase | Dates | Goal | Main activities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-launch | Nov to Dec 2026 | Waitlist and evidence | Waitlist page (US-007), 20 interviews (EXP-01), build-in-public posts, recruit G1 listeners, recruit 30 to 50 "founding creators" |
| Closed beta | From 11 Jan 2027 (A-003) | Keep rate and retention | Invite waitlist in weekly cohorts of 100 to 300; private community channel; weekly feedback calls; founding-creator showcase |
| Paid launch (India) | After gate G3 | First revenue | Open payments; founding-creator discount for the first year [H]; launch week with creator collaborations |
| Wave 2 | After evidence of fit in India | Expansion | Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka; Indonesia; Brazil (market analysis 4.3) |

## 3. Channels (zero-budget first)

| Channel | Why it fits | Tactic | Owner | Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Creator communities (Discord, Telegram, Reddit groups for Indian YouTubers and editors) | Where creators ask for background music | Helpful answers, free founding-creator invites, no spam | Founder | Waitlist sign-ups by source |
| Short-form content (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels) | The product makes music for exactly this format | "Made in 60 seconds" videos with the AI label shown; Hindi and English | Founder plus one developer per week | Views to sign-up rate |
| Search (SEO) | Creators search for background music | Pages for intents such as "background music for vlog in Hindi", "wedding reel music" with honest wording | Developer C | Organic sign-ups |
| Founding-creator programme | Social proof and feedback | 30 to 50 creators get free Pro during beta in return for feedback and a credit line | Founder | Retention of founding creators |
| College fests and music clubs | Independent artists and lyricists | Workshops: "Your lyrics to a song in 10 minutes" | Founder | Artist sign-ups |
| Video-editing tool and template creators | They need music for templates | Partnership: templates with BeatMind tracks and attribution | Founder | Partner-sourced sign-ups |
| Product Hunt and global AI directories | English worldwide reach | One launch at paid launch | Founder | Global sign-ups |

Paid acquisition waits until funding and a measured payback period.

## 4. Funnel and targets [H]

| Stage | Definition | Beta target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waitlist | Sign-ups with consent | 500 or more in 6 weeks (H-001) |
| Activation | Invited user keeps at least one take in week 1 | 50% |
| Keep rate | Takes kept / takes generated | 20% (METRIC-02) |
| Week-4 retention | Active in week 4 | 25% |
| Conversion | Free to paid within 30 days after payments open | Measured; scenario base case 2.5% a month |
| Referral | Invited users who invite another | 10% |

## 5. Pricing at launch

Prices from the [pricing and financial model](pricing-and-financial-model.md): ₹149 Creator and ₹349 Pro in India; US$8 and US$18 in band B1. Launch offer for founding creators and early payers: first 3 months at a discount [H], always shown with the regular price and end date (UX spec section 6).
